The Homegrowns Cafe

A complete cafe website

Screens

About the project

The Homegrowns is a website built for The Homegrowns Cafe. It helps customers find out about the cafe, book events and buy merchandise, and it helps the cafe share news and promotions.

What it can do

  • Cafe overview: information about the cafe, its atmosphere and what it offers.
  • Event booking: an online way to reserve events and gatherings.
  • Merchandise shop: browse and buy cafe merchandise.
  • News feed: the latest news, promotions and announcements.
  • Menu: a full menu sorted into categories, with prices.
  • About us: the story behind The Homegrowns.

What I learned

This project taught me how to build a complete business website with many pages and features. I learned how online shops, booking systems and content updates work when a real business depends on them.
